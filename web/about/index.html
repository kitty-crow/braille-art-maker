How it works

PNG to Unicode Braille.

Vectorise

The pinned Vectoriser converts the source to path-only SVG with cropping disabled. Braille Art Maker rasterises that SVG for the remaining stages.

Tone and dither

The image is resized to the Braille dot grid, converted to alpha-aware luminance, stretched and sharpened. Ordered 4×4 with inverted polarity is the default; Atkinson, Floyd-Steinberg and threshold modes are also available.

Colour

Foreground colour samples the source pixels represented by raised dots. Background colour separately preserves off-dot pixels. Full colour treats each 2×4 cell as a two-colour tile: the eight source samples are grouped perceptually, the grouping becomes the Braille character and the two representative colours become foreground and background. Uniform cells use one colour for both, giving a solid-looking text cell.

Pack and render

Each 2×4 binary block maps to one Unicode Braille character. The dense renderer measures the active Braille font and fixes cell geometry to a 1:2 character ratio. Colour TXT uses foreground/background tags and self-contained HTML writes the colours directly into the cells.
